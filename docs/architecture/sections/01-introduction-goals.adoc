== Introduction and Goals

Sammlarr is a self-hosted https://learn.microsoft.com/aspnet/core/blazor[Blazor Server]
app that builds curated lists of movies and shows. It has no catalogue of its own. It pulls
from external services and stitches the results into one local library: trending titles and
metadata from TMDB, list contents from MDBList, and a watchlist it reads from and writes back
to Plex. IMDb and TheTVDB identifiers tie the same title together across all of them.

The app is multi-user but small. Each person signs in with their Plex account and keeps their
own feeds, watchlist and blacklist on a shared instance.

=== Requirements Overview

The user-facing feature areas. Most map to a folder under `UI/Features`:

[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===
| Feature | What it does

| Browse | Browse trending movies and shows from TMDB and add them to the watchlist.
| Feeds | Define and preview a _feed_: a saved query over an external provider list (an MDBList list) with filter, sort and limit rules.
| Watchlist | The watchlist Sammlarr keeps in sync with Plex, pulling remote changes and pushing local ones through a durable outbox, with per-item push status.
| Home | The local library view of the current watchlist.
| Blacklist | Exclude specific media so they never appear in curated lists.
| Notifications | Webhook and Pushover notifications for watchlist and sync events, bundled and templated (configured under Settings).
| Auth | Plex OAuth sign-in; app-owned users with Admin/Member roles and open or closed registration.
| Settings | Manage providers, secrets (API keys and credentials), users, notifications, caching and diagnostics.
|===

NOTE: Feeds can be defined and previewed today. The background scheduler that would run them
on a cron and reconcile them into the watchlist is planned, not built yet (see
<<section-technical-risks>>). For now items enter the watchlist by manual add or Plex sync.

=== Quality Goals

The qualities that shape the design, most important first. Each is a top node of the quality
tree in <<section-quality-scenarios>>.

[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===
| Goal | Motivation

| Correctness of list building | Feeds and the watchlist must resolve to the _right_ movies and shows. A bad match quietly corrupts the whole library.
| Resilience of provider calls | External providers rate-limit and fail. A 429 storm or an outage must not take down an entire run.
| Per-user data isolation | Several people share one instance. Each must only ever see and change their own feeds, watchlist and blacklist.
| Maintainability | One person evolves the code. Strong typing, clear layering and source generation keep that tractable.
|===

=== Stakeholders

[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===
| Role | Expectation

| Admin | The first user to sign in. Configures providers, secrets, users and registration mode; has full Settings access.
| Member | Any later user. Curates their own feeds, watchlist and blacklist; no administrative settings.
| Maintainer | The single developer who builds and operates Sammlarr; wants a low-ceremony, well-documented architecture.
|===

The significant technical choices are captured as short decision records (ADRs). See the
link:https://github.com/daerup/Sammlarr/blob/main/docs/adr/README.md[architecture decision log].
